Maybe a difficult situation came up with an employee or leader, and you want to talk it through before deciding what to do next.

That is what Founder Advisory is designed to provide.

Founder Advisory is often the relationship founders move into after completing a Company Redesign with Orbsi.

By that point, we already understand how the company works, the people involved, the decisions that brought you here, where you are trying to go, and the role you want to have in the future.

The redesign may be complete, but the company will continue to change.

People leave. Leaders take on more responsibility. New opportunities appear. Problems come up that no process could have predicted. What you want from the company may change too.

Not every situation requires another redesign.

Sometimes you need someone who already understands the company and can help you think through what comes next.

An Ongoing Relationship for Leading the Company

Founder Leadership Development focuses on how you lead people.

Founder Advisory supports you as you make decisions about the company itself.

The conversation may involve a difficult employee situation, a major hire, a new opportunity, a change in direction, another location, a new department, or a shift in the role you want to have.

Sometimes the situation will require you to grow as a leader, but the relationship is not limited to leadership development.

You have someone who already knows the company and can help you think through what is happening, what it could affect, and what should happen next.

Founder Advisory is intentionally limited to a small number of clients.

You will work directly with Danielle, allowing her to stay close enough to understand your company and be useful when important situations come up.

Depending on the situation, timing, and location, that may include a last-minute call, an in-person meeting, or Danielle joining a conversation that needs to happen quickly.

What Founder Advisory May Look Like

The situations founders face are not always predictable, so the relationship is designed to adjust to what is happening.

Sometimes you may need a scheduled conversation to work through a larger decision.

Sometimes something happens unexpectedly and you want to call before responding.

Sometimes it may make sense for Danielle to meet with you in person, join a conversation, or help you work through a situation as it unfolds.

Founder Advisory may include:

  • Scheduled advisory conversations
  • Calls when something unexpected comes up
  • Talking through a decision before you make it
  • Reviewing an idea, plan, or opportunity
  • Preparing for a difficult employee or leadership conversation
  • Meeting in person when the situation calls for it and location allows
  • Thinking through how a decision could affect other parts of the company
  • Revisiting your priorities, direction, or role as things change

The support depends on what is happening and what would be most useful at the time.

Founder Advisory May Be Right for You If...

  • You have completed a Company Redesign and want to continue working with someone who already understands your company.
  • You regularly face decisions that affect your people, company, or future and want another experienced perspective.
  • You want someone you can call without having to explain the company from the beginning every time.
  • You are considering a new location, department, service, business model, or opportunity and want help thinking through the decision.
  • You are dealing with a difficult employee or leadership situation and want to consider your options before responding.
  • You want the relationship to adjust to what is happening rather than follow the same agenda every time you meet.
  • You value having someone who will question your assumptions, tell you what they see, and help you consider what a decision could affect.
  • You want an ongoing relationship with someone who can stay close to the company as it changes.

Founder Advisory May Not Be Right for You If...

  • You are looking for someone to make decisions for you.
  • You only want agreement or reassurance rather than an honest outside perspective.
  • Your company needs to be redesigned, not simply supported through individual decisions and situations.
  • You primarily want help with how you communicate, delegate, give feedback, or lead your team. Founder Leadership Development may be a better fit.
  • You want someone to take over the work rather than help you think through it and lead it.
