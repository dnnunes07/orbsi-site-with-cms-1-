This probably feels familiar.

People still come to you for answers.
You hired managers, but they still come to you before making decisions.
You're reviewing work that someone else should be responsible for.
You spend more time solving problems than preventing them from happening.
You hired people expecting some relief, but somehow things feel more overwhelming than they did before.
You can physically leave the business, but mentally you're still there.

This isn't unusual. It's a pattern we see as founder-led companies grow.

Your business changed. The company didn't.

There was a time when the way you operated made sense.

  • The team was small.
  • Decisions were made quickly.
  • You knew everything that was happening.
  • People came directly to you because they knew you'd have the answer.

Then your business grew.

More employees. More customers. More locations. More complexity. More responsibility.

Every time the business grew, you adapted to absorb it.

  • You answered more questions.
  • Made more decisions.
  • Solved more problems.
  • Held more context.
  • Filled more gaps.

More and more responsibility simply kept collecting around one person.

This is not because you're controlling, don't fully trust your team, or hired the wrong people. It is because the company never changed to support what the business became. This pattern has a name and a fix. More on why the work keeps coming back and what company redesign changes.

Companies don't naturally evolve as they grow. They have to be intentionally redesigned.

What is company redesign?

Company redesign is intentionally changing how the company is built so it can support what the business has become and what's next.

It means deciding:

  • Who makes decisions.
  • Who owns what.
  • How work gets handed off.
  • How people know what's expected.
  • How leaders lead.
  • How standards stay consistent.
  • How performance gets addressed.
  • How compensation is structured.
  • What should still depend on you.
  • And what shouldn't.

Building a company requires more than better systems.

It requires three things to evolve together.

1

The Company

The company evolves to support more people, more complexity, and the next stage of the business.

2

The Leaders

Your leaders take responsibility for decisions, team development, and the work that should no longer depend on you.

3

The Founder

The founder learns to build the company instead of continuing to absorb the growth themselves.

When these three evolve together, the business gains the capacity to continue growing without becoming increasingly dependent on one person.

We don't redesign companies to create growth.

Your business has already grown. Growth is what exposes the need for a redesign.

Company redesign creates capacity.
Capacity creates choices.

  • To continue growing.
  • To step back.
  • To prepare for succession.
  • To eventually sell.

How We Work

Every founder's business is different, so we don't begin with solutions just yet. We begin with understanding.

01 Phase 01

Define the destination

Where is the business going, and what role do you want to play in it?

02 Phase 02

Understand the current reality

How work actually happens today, and where decisions live.

03 Phase 03

Diagnose what still depends on you

Whether the company is missing structure, authority, leadership capability, context, standards, or support.

04 Phase 04

Design the company solution

The structure, leadership, and ways of working the next stage requires.

05 Phase 05

Implement and embed

Put the design into practice and help you, your leaders, and your team begin working differently.

06 Phase 06

Observe, coach, and refine

See what holds, what does not, and what needs to evolve as the company changes.
