Leadership Development

Leadership is learned.

A title can give someone authority and responsibility. It does not automatically prepare them to lead.

Founders and leaders are expected to make hard decisions, communicate expectations, address performance, navigate conflict, and guide other people, often without ever being taught how.

Some are leading people for the first time. Others have been leading for years, but their role and what the company needs from them have changed.

Orbsi helps founders and leaders prepare for the decisions, conversations, and responsibilities their roles require.

We do not believe in generic leadership training.

We focus on what the person is facing, what their role requires, and where they need help.

You do not have to develop every leader in your company yourself.

You may feel like helping your leaders grow should be your responsibility.

But you may not have the time or experience to prepare someone for a new role or help them with every part of leading people.

That does not mean their growth has to stop or that you have to figure it out alone.

Orbsi works directly with you or someone on your team around the real people, decisions, conversations, and responsibilities they are handling inside the company.

That may include helping them:

  • Communicate expectations clearly
  • Prepare for difficult conversations
  • Address performance concerns sooner
  • Give useful feedback
  • Delegate without losing track of the work
  • Navigate conflict
  • Lead more productive meetings
  • Set clearer boundaries
  • Stop stepping in too quickly
  • Take on more responsibility

You can stay involved in your leaders' growth without being the only person responsible for it.

Leadership Development May Be Right for You If...

  • You were never taught how to lead a growing company and want help giving clearer direction, delegating, giving feedback, or holding people responsible.
  • You promoted someone because they were dependable or good at the work, but they are struggling to lead other people.
  • You have a manager who avoids difficult conversations, waits too long to address problems, or brings too many decisions back to you.
  • You know you need to hire a right-hand person, but you are not sure where to begin, what experience and qualities to look for, or how to tell which candidate is the right fit—and you want another experienced person involved in the hiring process.
  • You are considering someone already on your team for the right-hand role, but you want another experienced perspective before deciding whether they are truly ready for the responsibility.
  • You want someone outside the company to work directly with a leader on something you do not have the time or experience to teach yourself.
  • You are not sure whether someone needs more guidance, clearer expectations, or a role that is a better fit.
  • You want your leaders to take on more without simply handing them more work and hoping they figure it out.

Leadership Development for You and Your Team

01

Founder Leadership Development(One-on-one coaching)

One-on-one support for founders who are learning how to lead through other people, not just execute the work themselves.

As your company grows, your role changes. You are no longer only responsible for having the idea, making the decision, or getting the work done. You also have to communicate the direction clearly, help other people understand why it matters, and get them to follow through.

That may mean learning how to:

  • turn an idea in your head into clear direction someone else can act on
  • get buy-in instead of simply telling people what to do
  • lead with kindness without becoming too passive
  • set expectations clearly and address it when they are not met
  • hold people responsible without taking the work back over
  • stop letting difficult conversations go on too long
  • change your focus as more of the day-to-day work moves to other people
  • spend less time executing and more time directing, developing, and deciding

The work helps you lead from the role you want to have now while preparing for the role you want in the future.

02

One-on-One Leadership Development(For managers, executives, and people preparing to lead)

One-on-one support for people who are already leading others or preparing to take on more responsibility.

That may include helping them:

  • Communicate expectations clearly
  • Prepare for difficult conversations
  • Address performance concerns sooner
  • Give useful feedback
  • Delegate work without losing track of it
  • Navigate conflict between employees
  • Lead meetings that end with clear decisions and follow-through
  • Make decisions without bringing every question back to someone else
  • Coach employees instead of immediately solving the problem for them
  • Adjust as their role and responsibilities grow

The work is based on what they are actually facing, not a general leadership curriculum.
This service is also available to leaders working within corporate environments.

03

Right-Hand Hiring and Development(Hiring support + ongoing development)

Few hiring decisions have a greater impact on a founder's future than choosing the person who will become their right hand.

This person may become the connection between you and the rest of the company. The right person can lead managers, make decisions, keep people accountable, and take responsibility for work that previously came back to you.

The wrong person can create more confusion, slow decisions down, and pull you even further into the day-to-day.

Because this role matters so much, we help you determine what you need, evaluate the people you are considering, and prepare the person you choose for the role.

That may include helping you:

  • Determine whether you are ready for a right-hand person
  • Define what the person will be responsible for
  • Identify the experience and qualities they need
  • Decide whether someone already on your team could grow into the role
  • Evaluate internal and external candidates
  • Clarify which decisions they can make without you
  • Establish what should still come back to you
  • Decide how the two of you will communicate and work together
  • Support them as they begin taking on the role

Our involvement does not have to end once the person is hired or promoted. We can continue working directly with them as they begin leading others, making decisions, and taking responsibility for more of the company.

Standalone Leadership Development

Leadership Development is a one-on-one partnership focused on helping you become more prepared for the responsibilities your role requires. Whether you’re a founder, executive, manager, or an emerging leader, the work centers on the real situations you’re navigating today.

Right-Hand Hiring & Development

This work is for founders who know they need someone beside them to lead more of the company but are not sure where to begin, what the role should be, or who is truly ready for it.

Danielle can serve as another experienced set of eyes and ears throughout the hiring process, helping you review candidates, participate in selected interviews, notice potential bias, and separate a strong personal connection from whether someone is actually prepared for the responsibility.

The goal is not simply to fill the position. It is to choose the right person, define the role clearly, and help them become capable of carrying the responsibility you hired them to take on.

Cadence

A common development cadence is four 60-minute sessions each month. The exact cadence depends on the hiring stage, the person’s readiness, and whether the work is part of a company redesign.
